# Mangi Store — Business Management System (POS)

## Project
A POS and business management system to digitize sales operations for Mwanga Enterprise, Arusha,
Tanzania (2025-2026).

## Situation (Problem)
Mwanga Enterprise relied heavily on handwritten sales records, manual inventory tracking, and
physical calculation of daily business transactions. This caused stock inconsistencies, calculation
mistakes, delayed reporting, and slow customer service during busy business hours.

## Task
Develop a POS and business management system to digitize sales operations, automate calculations,
improve inventory tracking, and simplify daily business management.

## Action (Solution)
- Designed and developed a full-stack POS platform with inventory management, automated
  transaction calculations, sales monitoring, receipt generation, and analytics dashboards.
- Implemented structured reporting workflows to simplify stock management, financial tracking, and
  day-to-day business operations.
- Built with frontend + backend integration (React.js, Node.js), transaction processing, and
  PostgreSQL-style database design.

## Result (Impact)
- Reduced daily sales recording and manual calculation time by approximately 4 hours per day.
- Improved inventory tracking efficiency by 70%.
- Minimized stock and calculation errors significantly.
- Increased transaction processing speed during peak business hours through automated sales and reporting.
